## AI help remove complexities of DeFi

The DeFi ecosystem is complex and optimised yield generation is sophisticated and inaccessible to average users. While expert yield farmers and institutions leverage complex strategies with privileged information, most participants lack the tools and knowledge to optimize their returns. Artificial intelligence serves as the great equalizer, capable of analyzing market conditions, discovering opportunities, and executing strategies with efficiency that matches or exceeds human traders.

At its core, Voltr provides the base infrastructure for AI agents and builders to deploy, manage, and optimize yield strategies across Solana's DeFi landscape. By standardizing core vault functionality while enabling complete strategy customization, the protocol creates a foundation where sophisticated yield generation becomes accessible to all participants through data-driven automation.
